## Global runtime rules

These are implementation contracts. Prior cloud-browser checks and the unrun final-integration/cross-browser tests are distinguished in `ASTRA_V5_QA.md`; this list is not a claim that every fallback passed in a browser.

Native scroll, no wheel hijack. Offscreen/hidden-tab/manual pause/dialog states stop ambient video and CSS motion. Reduced motion and Save-Data begin with static assets and no assigned ambient video src. Failed autoplay retains a still. User-requested film opens separately and pauses on close. Nothing saves a habit or transmits medical data.

## S03 motion scientific guardrails

The approved image is a conceptual LDL cutaway, with deliberately nonliteral shape/color/scale. It is not a cell, atomistic model, HDL reconstruction or measurable concentration. No membrane opening, extrusion of free cholesterol, cargo escape, LDL-to-HDL morph, counting of the photographed blobs, therapeutic shrinking, or visual plaque removal. ApoB is a separate graphic explanation of atherogenic particle number, including particles beyond LDL.

A2D optical study can test buoyancy, slight whole-image tilt, focus and lighting rhythm. It must be named as such; it is not true3D rotation or generated natural video. A paid image-to-video test, if proposed, must lock the silhouette, cutaway, cargo and palette and remain within explicitly approved model/duration/attempt/credits. Final scientific acceptance is separate from visual polish.

## Shot-level acceptance criteria

- Meaning is visible before movement; reduced-motion frame remains strong.
- Motion has a reason and endpoint; no endless identical parallax.
- Faces, age, fingers, props and wardrobe stay stable in future natural shots.
- Scientific animation does not assert a new causal claim or magnitude.
- Package labels remain ordinary editable text with stable perspective.
- All process symbols are editorial, never individual medical predictions.
- Both the separate 26 s foyer film and original 26 s animatic use crop-designed source shots rather than complete presentation screens. The foyer film uses accepted motion ranges; the original animatic remains a distinct optical concept.
